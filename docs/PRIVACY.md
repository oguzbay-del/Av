# Av Haritası — Privacy policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026 · [Türkçe](GIZLILIK.md)

Av Haritası is an independent, unofficial app. It has no accounts, ads, analytics or third-party tracking SDKs.
The developer runs no server and receives no data about you.

## Data that stays on your device

- **Location:** used on the device to work out your status on the hunting area map. Location history is never
  sent to a server. If you start a track recording, the track is stored only on the device; GPX export and
  location sharing go only to the app or person you choose.
- **Hunting permit:** the image or PDF you add is read on the device (Apple Vision / PDFKit). The hunting area,
  day, species and quotas are stored; names and document/card numbers are not.
- **Bag log, settings, diagnostics:** stored on the device only. Diagnostic reports (MetricKit) are sent only if
  you share them.

## Data that leaves your device

| When | Where | What is sent |
|---|---|---|
| Weather and wind forecast | [Open-Meteo](https://open-meteo.com) | Your coordinates (4 decimal places, about 10 m) |
| Map tiles (if the OSM / Topo base map is selected) | OpenStreetMap, OpenTopoMap | Addresses of the map tiles you view |
| Apple map, search, directions | Apple (MapKit) / Google Maps if you choose it | Under Apple's or Google's own policies |
| Bird sound identification (only if you entered a server address and tap the button) | The BirdNET server **you configure** | A 15-second recording, coordinates, week of the year |

None of this is linked to an account or identity.

## Permissions

- **Location (While Using):** the status bar and alerts on the map.
- **Location (Always):** only if you turn on "Alert even when the app is closed" in Settings, to warn you near a
  no-hunting zone.
- **Microphone:** only when you tap the button in the Bird Sound tab.
- **Notifications:** no-hunting zone alerts.
- **Local network:** if your bird sound server runs on a computer at home.

You can revoke any permission in iPhone Settings › Av Haritası. Deleting the app deletes all of its data on the device.

## Children

The app is intended for hunters and does not knowingly collect data from children.
