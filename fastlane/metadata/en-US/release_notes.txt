First release of Av Haritası (Hunting Map):
- Istanbul 2026-2027 hunting area map with a live "OK to hunt / caution / do not hunt" status based on the MAK decree
- Alerts when approaching a no-hunting zone (even when the app is closed)
- Today: hunting day, legal hours, open species, weather and wind, daily bag log
- Reads your hunting permit on-device and gives directions to your hunting area
- Bird species guess from sound (BirdNET)
- Live hunting status on the Lock Screen and Apple Watch
This app is not official; the hunter remains legally responsible.
