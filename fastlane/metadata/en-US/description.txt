Av Haritası (Hunting Map) shows whether the place and time where you are hunting in Istanbul are allowed under the 2026-2027 hunting season decision of Turkey's Central Hunting Commission (MAK). It warns you when you approach or enter a no-hunting zone.

IMPORTANT: This app is unofficial and is not affiliated with the Ministry of Agriculture and Forestry or any government body. Zone boundaries were extracted from the scanned official map and are approximate. The official MAK decision published in the Official Gazette and the official map prevail; the hunter remains legally responsible. Currently only Istanbul province is covered.

MAP
• 2026-27 Istanbul hunting area map: no-hunting zones, protected areas, wildlife release sites, state, general and model hunting areas
• Live status bar at the top: OK to hunt / Caution / Do not hunt. Tap it to see every rule check
• Distance rules such as 300 m from villages, picnic areas and highways, closed hunting areas, hunting days and legal hours
• Distance and direction to the nearest no-hunting zone, with a dashed line to the boundary and a GPS accuracy circle on the map, rotated with the compass
• Alerts even when the app is closed: iOS wakes the app as you approach a no-hunting zone (battery friendly)
• Long-press the map to check a spot before you go
• Base maps: Apple satellite and standard, OpenTopoMap (contours, trails), OpenStreetMap; viewed tiles are kept on the device for offline use
• Wind badge and scent cone: see where the wind carries your scent

TODAY
• Is today a hunting day, and the legal hours for your location (1 hour before sunrise to 1 hour after sunset)
• Species open today and daily bag limits, next hunting days, season dates
• Weather and wind (Open-Meteo): direction, speed, gusts, temperature, precipitation, pressure trend
• Today's bag: a counter per species with MAK limits applied; stored only on your device

IN THE FIELD
• Waypoints: car, blind, downed game, water and notes; offline compass guidance with direction, distance and walking time, GPX sharing
• Field mode: large buttons and status text for use with gloves
• Red night theme: a dark, red-tinted interface and dimmed map protect your night vision; can switch on automatically at sunset
• Siri and Shortcuts: ask Av Haritası whether you can hunt here or when legal hours end

HUNTING AREA AND PERMIT
• Add a screenshot, photo or PDF of your hunting permit; it is read on the device (area, day, species, quotas). Names and document numbers are not stored
• Your hunting area is highlighted on the map, with directions to its nearest edge in Apple Maps or Google Maps

RULES
• 2026-27 changes for Istanbul, distance limits, hunting areas, protected areas and key prohibitions, each with its article number. The Turkish text is the legally valid one

BIRD ID (SOUND AND PHOTO)
• Sound: listens for 15 seconds and guesses the species, then matches it against the MAK lists: huntable today, out of season, prohibited or protected
• Sound runs entirely on the phone with Cornell Lab's BirdNET model, limited to the species of Istanbul; no internet needed
• Photo: the photo you pick is analysed entirely on the phone (Vision / Core ML) and never uploaded

ALSO
• Live hunting status on the Lock Screen, Dynamic Island and Apple Watch (optional)
• Track recording and GPX export, location sharing, 112 emergency card
• Turkish and English interface

PRIVACY
No account, no ads, no tracking. Location history is never sent to a server. Bird sounds and photos are analysed on the phone and never uploaded. Only approximate coordinates are sent to Open-Meteo for the weather forecast.
